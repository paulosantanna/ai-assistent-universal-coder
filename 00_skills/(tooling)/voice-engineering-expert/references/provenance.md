# Provenance

Updated: 2026-11-03.

The super-skill is an instruction package of `codenavi-agent`. The executable knowledge and the synthesizer live in `audio-engineering-mcp/`. Notes in `corpus.py` are original summaries. Each topic points at public pages consulted on 2026-11-03, including MIT OpenCourseWare 6.003, RES.6-008 and 6.341, the MIT Media Lab KEMAR set, Stanford CCRMA, EBU R 128, ITU-R BS.1770, AES, Sound On Sound, Shure, ISO 3382-1, Praat, Klatt 1980 and 1990, Moore and Glasberg 1983, Escudero et al. 2009, Barbosa 2008, Hillenbrand et al. 1995, W3C SSML 1.1, eSpeak NG and Opus.

No standard, textbook or paper body is vendored. eSpeak NG is cited as the public description of the formant-synthesis ceiling; its code is not imported. There is no neural vocoder and no recorded donor.
