# Safety

Read this before any render.

## Allowed

Original parametric voices for narration, applications, games, accessibility and education. Presentations: masculine or feminine. Ages: child, adult, older. Languages: pt-BR and en-US.

## Refused

- Donor audio, reference recording, clone, clonar, impersonation or a named person to imitate.
- Fraud, scam, phishing, golpe or a fake emergency.
- Sexual or pornographic use at any age.
- A child profile with an empty use context.

The renderer raises a refusal and writes no WAV. Do not route the same request to a commercial voice API.

## Child profiles

`age=child` is an acoustic preset: higher F0 and a shorter-tract formant scale. It exists for non-sexual narration. Pass a concrete use context such as "narração educacional" or "fala de jogo". A sexual word anywhere in the text or the context refuses the render.
