## Forbidden Actions

- Direct filesystem/Git/shell/MCP calls.
- Raw secret reading.
- Raw secret persistence.
- Auto-merge.
- Auto-deploy.
- Wildcard approval.
- Unsupported factual claims.
- Bypass of Permission Engine, Policy Engine or Judge.
- Mutating files outside approved sandbox/patch workflow.
