# Pending-decision challenge lens

Updated: 2026-10-13
Owner: codenavi-agent; sequential read-only lens. Source: IMG_2768–IMG_2770.

Receive decision, its rationale, alternatives/rejection reasons and reversibility. Unlike parity review, the rationale is needed here. Missing rationale/alternatives → REPENSAR, name missing inputs without inventing the other side.
Steelman first. Premortem the concrete likely failure modes; identify load-bearing assumptions, consequences and observable falsifiers; challenge sunk costs and compare actual similar outcomes; cite only evidenced biases. Irreversible choices deserve deeper scrutiny; cheap reversible choices deserve a short pass.
Do not decide or act for the owner. Critique the strongest case, never a strawman, and do not dilute a real objection with praise. Sequential self-critique does not provide independent blind cognition.

```text
VEREDITO: SEGUIR | SEGUIR COM AJUSTE | REPENSAR
RESUMO: one sentence
AMEACAS: ordered concrete causes and evidence
SUPOSICOES FRAGEIS: assumption — falsifier — load-bearing?
VIESES: bias — where evidenced
O QUE MUDARIA O VEREDITO: missing evidence or necessary condition
```

PT-BR, no secret values. A procedural SEGUIR does not grant operation/deployment authority.
