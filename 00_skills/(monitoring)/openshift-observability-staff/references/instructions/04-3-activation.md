## 3. Activation

Activate when:

- the user requests deep OpenShift observability, monitoring, logging, operator, alert, route or cluster troubleshooting analysis;
- the playbook `analayze-metricas` requires an isolated OpenShift analysis;
- an incident report mentions OpenShift telemetry, dashboards, alerts, service impact, events, logs, traces or monitoring gaps.
