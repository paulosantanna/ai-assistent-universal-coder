## 3. Activation

Activate when:

- the user requests deep Splunk, ITSI, Observability Cloud, SPL, episode, notable event, metric, log or trace analysis;
- the playbook `analayze-metricas` requires an isolated Splunk analysis;
- an incident report mentions Splunk telemetry, dashboards, alerts, service impact, events, logs, traces or monitoring gaps.
