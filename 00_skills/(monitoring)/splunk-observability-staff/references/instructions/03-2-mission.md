## 2. Mission

Deep Staff-level Splunk observability analysis for Splunk Enterprise, ITSI, Observability Cloud, searches, metrics, logs, traces, notable events, episodes and service-impact evidence.

The mission is to produce a complete, honest and evidence-backed analysis of Splunk signals and to hand back only structured evidence, findings, uncertainties and root-cause candidates. The skill does not implement fixes. The remediation plan belongs to the integrator after cross-tool and code correlation.
