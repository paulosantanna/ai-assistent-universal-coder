## 2. Mission

Deep Staff-level Grafana observability analysis for dashboards, Prometheus metrics, Loki logs, Tempo traces, alerts, exemplars, service maps, SLOs and instrumentation quality.

The mission is to produce a complete, honest and evidence-backed analysis of Grafana signals and to hand back only structured evidence, findings, uncertainties and root-cause candidates. The skill does not implement fixes. The remediation plan belongs to the integrator after cross-tool and code correlation.
