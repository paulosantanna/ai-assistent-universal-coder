## 2. Mission

Deep Staff-level Dynatrace observability analysis for metrics, logs, traces, Davis problems, topology, Kubernetes context, SLOs and cost-safe root-cause evidence.

The mission is to produce a complete, honest and evidence-backed analysis of Dynatrace signals and to hand back only structured evidence, findings, uncertainties and root-cause candidates. The skill does not implement fixes. The remediation plan belongs to the integrator after cross-tool and code correlation.
