## 3. Activation

Activate when:

- the user requests deep Dynatrace observability, Davis, problem, metric, trace, log or service-impact analysis;
- the playbook `analayze-metricas` requires an isolated Dynatrace analysis;
- an incident report mentions Dynatrace telemetry, dashboards, alerts, service impact, events, logs, traces or monitoring gaps.
