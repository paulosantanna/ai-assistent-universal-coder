# NEGATIVE_KNOWLEDGE.md

Known failures and unsafe shortcuts for `specs`:

- Do not let a downstream skill create or alter artifacts from a vague objective with no requirements.
- Do not treat task lists as a substitute for acceptance criteria.
- Do not mark test categories `NOT_APPLICABLE` because running them is inconvenient.
- Do not infer human approval from silence.
- Do not copy external process content verbatim into AEOS contracts.
