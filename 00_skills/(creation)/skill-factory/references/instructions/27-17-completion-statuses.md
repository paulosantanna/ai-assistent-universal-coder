## 17. Completion statuses

Use only:

- `GENERATED`
- `GENERATED_AND_VALIDATED`
- `REWORK_REQUIRED`
- `BLOCKED`
- `WAITING_APPROVAL`
- `FAILED_VALIDATION`

Do not claim `GENERATED_AND_VALIDATED` unless the validator exits successfully.
