# Domain adapter: finance and accounting

Applies when deliverables involve financial modeling, budget analysis, unit economics, forecasting, or audit schedules.

## Minimum evidence set (binding)

1. **Source financial statements or ledger records**.
2. **Formula definitions & assumptions list**.
3. **Currency, tax rate, and period boundaries**.

## Fraud table (for fable-judge)

| Fraud | Symptom |
|---|---|
| Broken spreadsheet formulas | hardcoded values in place of dynamic formulas |
| Hidden assumption shifts | changing discount rates or inflation without disclosure |
