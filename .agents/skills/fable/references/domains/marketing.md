# Domain adapter: marketing and content

Applies when deliverables involve copywriting, campaign strategy, SEO content, email sequencing, or brand positioning.

## Minimum evidence set (binding)

1. **Brand tone of voice & positioning guide**.
2. **Target audience ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**.
3. **Primary search intent / keyword metrics**.

## Fraud table (for fable-judge)

| Fraud | Symptom |
|---|---|
| Fluffy claims | unbacked superlatives ("best-in-class") |
| Keyword stuffing | un-natural density overriding user readability |
