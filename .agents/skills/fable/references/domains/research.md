# Domain adapter: research and reporting

Applies when the deliverable is an answer to a question about the world: market research, technical comparisons, policy/grant questions, recommendations, reports.

## Minimum evidence set (binding, before any conclusion)

1. **Current primary sources for every load-bearing figure**: official pages, documentation, filings, fetched during this task.
2. **At least two independent sources** for any figure the recommendation hinges on.
3. **A recency check**: confirm effective date for prices, versions, laws, schemes.

## Evidence and primary sources

Official sources over aggregators, aggregators over blogs, blogs over memory.

## Authority order

User constraints > primary sources > secondary sources > memory.

## Verification by observation

- Every claim traces to an opened, cited source.
- Arithmetic recomputed and shown.
- Links resolve to pages matching claims.
- "Could not verify" section exists when applicable.

## Fraud table (for fable-judge)

| Fraud | Symptom |
|---|---|
| Fabricated citations | sources that do not exist or do not support claim |
| Stale figures | old prices/rules stated as current |
| Impossible arithmetic | numbers violating stated inputs |
| Option-dump conclusions | "it depends" with no recommendation |
