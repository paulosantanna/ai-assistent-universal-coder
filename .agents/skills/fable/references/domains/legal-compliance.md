# Domain adapter: legal and compliance research

Applies when deliverables involve regulatory mapping, license checks, privacy policy alignment (GDPR, HIPAA, LGPD), or contract review support.

## Minimum evidence set (binding)

1. **Official statutory or regulatory text (official gazette/gov site)**.
2. **Current effective dates of laws/regulations**.
3. **Jurisdiction scope definition**.

## Fraud table (for fable-judge)

| Fraud | Symptom |
|---|---|
| Unqualified legal advice | stating definitive legal conclusions instead of compliance research |
| Outdated statutory citations | referencing repealed laws or old amendments |
