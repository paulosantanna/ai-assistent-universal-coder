# Domain adapter: business operations

Applies when deliverables involve operational workflows, SOPs, resource allocation, process maps, or org logistics.

## Minimum evidence set (binding)

1. **Current process documentation / SOP**.
2. **Resource & constraint bounds**.
3. **Stakeholder roles & responsibilities**.

## Fraud table (for fable-judge)

| Fraud | Symptom |
|---|---|
| Hand-waving workflows | steps lacking concrete owners or triggers |
| Unconstrained planning | assuming infinite bandwidth or budget |
