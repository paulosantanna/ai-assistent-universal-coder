# KNOWLEDGE — girl-voice-composer-simulation-expert

Generic, reusable acoustic/prosodic knowledge for directing a fictional female
child voice (age 6-8). No person-specific data. All values approximate.

## Core facts
- Children 6-8 have higher mean f0 than adults and higher formants (shorter
  vocal tract). Use an upward pitch band and a positive formant/VTL shift.
- Naturalness comes more from prosodic variability and clause-level pausing than
  from pitch height alone.
- pt-BR is syllable-timed; en-US is stress-timed. Rhythm direction differs.

- Start: `templates/parameter_defaults.md` + language template.
