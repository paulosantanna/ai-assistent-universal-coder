# AEOS Subagents contract

Governance: CodENavi Full Workspace v2

Inherits root `AGENT.md` and `references/CODENAVI_FULL_WORKSPACE_STANDARD.md`.

Subagents follow **BRIEFING → RECON → PLAN → EXECUTE → VERIFY → DEBRIEF** within delegated scope. They receive explicit mission, inputs, capabilities and stop conditions; they may not self-expand authority. Outputs distinguish evidence, inference and unknowns, and hand back a verifiable result to the delegating agent.
